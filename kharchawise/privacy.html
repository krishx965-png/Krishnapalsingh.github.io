Privacy & Data Handling

Status: Private beta. Updated October 2026. This page explains how the application currently operates; it is not a guarantee against every security risk.

What is stored?

Your account email and authentication information are handled by Supabase Auth. Your own transactions, category budgets and recurring schedule details are saved in Supabase PostgreSQL when you use the logged-in dashboard. We do not ask for your bank password, card number, PIN, or OTP.

Who can see your records?

Database row-level security policies are configured so authenticated users can access rows associated with their own Supabase account ID. The website stores only a public Supabase publishable key, not an administrator key. Website operators with authorized project administration access may still access or manage database information; database isolation does not hide data from authorized administrators.

How are records used?

Records are used to show your dashboard, budgets, transaction list, recurring schedule and requested downloads. The application does not sync with your bank or automatically initiate any payments. No paid subscription or advertisements are active.

Your downloads and deletion

While signed in, choose My data & privacy to download CSV files or a JSON backup of your saved financial records. The Delete all financial records function removes records in the three financial tables after confirmation. It does not delete your Supabase authentication account or guarantee removal of provider backups. You may need to contact the website owner to request deletion of the authentication account.

Security and limitations

Site content is published through GitHub Pages; financial records are not written to the public GitHub repository. Supabase handles authentication and data storage. HTTPS should be used on the live website. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Free-plan usage and account creation are subject to Supabase provider limits, and account activation emails require suitable SMTP configuration for public sign-ups.

Your responsibility

Keep your account credentials private, use a unique password, download backups to a secure device, and never enter banking secrets in transaction notes.

KharchaWise is a personal budgeting and tracking tool, not a bank, lender, investment adviser or payment service.
