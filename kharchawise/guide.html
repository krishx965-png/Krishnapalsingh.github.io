How to use KharchaWise

Completely free, personal finance tracking. No credit card needed. Account confirmation emails are limited until the website owner enables a verified SMTP service.

  1. Create a free account: Enter your email and a strong unique password. Confirm your email if Supabase asks.
  2. Sign in: Once you are verified, use the same email and password on any device with a modern browser.
  3. Add transaction: Click + Add transaction; choose income/expense, date, category and amount.
  4. Monthly budget: Open Budgets, enter category limits. Review your spending and monthly summary.
  5. Recurring: Record repeating bills or salary reminders. Applying a schedule records a transaction; it does not make a payment.
  6. Export only your data: Open My data & privacy. Download JSON backup or CSV transactions, budgets and recurring items.
  7. Restore: Upload your own KharchaWise JSON backup. Review the counts before confirming. Existing matching records may be updated.
  8. Delete records: Download a backup, then use the Delete all financial records action. This does not delete your login account.

Privacy

Other users should not see your private records. Sign out on shared devices. Never enter PINs, passwords, card numbers, OTPs or sensitive banking credentials as transaction descriptions.

First login not working?

Check spam, confirm the account email, and ask the website owner to configure Supabase Email SMTP and Auth Redirect URL. Public email sign-ups cannot reliably work through Supabase's restricted default mail service.
