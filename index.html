WORK / FIELD PRACTICE & IMPACT

What I do.
How it supports decisions.

Monitoring, verification, reporting and team support across education programs.

01 / MONITORING & EVALUATION

Understand program progress

Review enrolment, retention, learning sessions and field coverage. Compare progress against plans and identify where follow-up is needed.

02 / DATA QUALITY

Check the evidence

Verify records against field observations and source documents, review mismatches and support data corrections through the appropriate team process.

03 / ANALYSIS & REPORTING

Make findings useful

Prepare Excel/MIS summaries, district and block comparisons, rankings and root-cause analysis. Connect each finding with a practical action.

04 / TEAM & FIELD SUPPORT

Help teams improve implementation

Support orientations, training, field reviews and follow-up conversations. Communicate findings in simple Hindi or clear English.
